Two Skies

Catch the moment. Pass it on.

Take a real photo of what you see right now and receive a moment from somewhere else in the world. No profiles, no followers, no exact location.

Contact

Questions, problems, or something that should not be in the app? Write to fligr.patrik@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 24 hours.

Report a photo

Open the photo, tap ... and choose Report. The photo disappears for you right away and is reviewed by a person. You can also Block sender there; they are not told.

Frequently asked

Who sees my photo? One other person, after a person on our side has checked it. They see the photo, your local time and your continent - never who you are.

Is my location shared? No. The app does not use GPS. Your continent comes from your phone's time zone, and all photo metadata (including GPS) is removed on your phone before sending.

Can the other person keep my photo? Yes. Whoever receives your moment can keep it, so only send what you are happy to give away.

How do I delete my data? See Deleting your data.
