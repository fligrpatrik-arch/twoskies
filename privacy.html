Two Skies

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026. This version covers the invite-only beta.

Two Skies lets you send a photo of what you see right now and receive one from somebody else, anonymously. We collect as little as possible to make that work.

Who is responsible

Patrik Flígr, contact: fligr.patrik@gmail.com. This person is the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • No GPS or precise location. The app never asks for location access.
  • No advertising identifiers, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools.
  • No access to your photo library. The camera is used only while you take a photo in the app; saving a photo to Photos asks only for permission to add photos.

What we collect and why

  • An anonymous account ID, created automatically the first time you send a photo. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple / Google account. Purpose: to deliver photos, apply the daily limit and let you block people.
  • The photos you send. Before a photo leaves your phone the app resizes it and removes all metadata (EXIF, GPS, camera model). Purpose: to show it to one other person.
  • Context shown with your photo: your local time when you sent it (for example 06:40), your local date (for the daily limit), and your country and continent, worked out from your phone's time zone (for example Europe/Prague means Czechia). Never a city, a place or GPS - nothing more precise than a whole country.
  • Exchanges and reactions: which photo went to which anonymous account, the reaction chosen and the part of day it was sent (morning, afternoon, evening, night). Purpose: to tell you where your moment went and how it was received.
  • Reports and blocks you make, and moderation decisions about your photos. Purpose: keeping the app safe.
  • Technical logs (such as IP address and time of a request), kept briefly by our hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.

Legal basis: performance of the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and working (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Who can see what

  • The person who receives your photo sees the photo, your local time and your country (shown as the whole country on a small globe). Never your account ID or anything that identifies you. In return, the person whose photo you receive learns that it reached someone in your country, and your reaction and part of the day if you react.
  • Our moderator checks the first photos of every new account before anyone receives them, and reviews reported photos. The moderator sees the photo and anonymous statistics (how many photos an account sent and how many were reported), not who you are. Photos from accounts in good standing go out without waiting.
  • A check on your phone looks for faces up close and for contact details (phone numbers, emails, handles, links) before a photo is sent, using your phone's own system (Apple Vision on iPhone, Google ML Kit on Android). It runs entirely on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere for it, and a photo that does not pass simply stays with you.
  • Please note: whoever receives your photo can keep it, save it or take a screenshot. Only send what you are happy to give away.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database, photo storage and anonymous sign-in). Our data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany). Supabase, Inc. is based in the USA and may process data under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. The app is distributed through Apple (App Store / TestFlight) and Google (Google Play), which have their own privacy policies.

On your phone

Your archive of exchanges (your photos, the photos you received and your reactions) is stored only on your phone. It is removed when you tap Settings → Delete account or delete the app.

How long we keep data and deleting your data

Our server is only the post office for photos. A photo you receive is saved on your phone as soon as it arrives, and your own photos stay on your phone. We then delete the photo files from our server automatically:

  • 14 days after the photo was delivered,
  • 30 days after the last report, if the photo was reported,
  • 7 days after it was sent, if it did not pass our review,
  • 1 day after upload, if the exchange never happened.

Photos waiting to be delivered stay until they are. The exchange records (which anonymous account sent and received what, reactions, local time and country) are kept for as long as the beta runs and deleted no later than 30 days after it ends.

Tap Settings → Delete account in the app. This deletes the photos you sent and all data linked to your anonymous account from our server, and your archive from your phone. People who already received your photos may have kept them. If you can no longer use the app (for example you deleted it), your anonymous account cannot be linked to you and its data is deleted together with the rest of the beta data; you can also write to us.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing and to data portability. Write to fligr.patrik@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz) or the data protection authority in your country.

Age

Two Skies is only for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
