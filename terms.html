Terms of Use

Last updated: 30 September 2026. These terms cover the invite-only beta.

The quiet rules

Two Skies has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. By using the app you agree to:

  • Send only photos you took yourself, in the app, of what you see.
  • Places, not faces: no people in close-up and no photos of strangers without their consent.
  • No nudity or sexual content, no violence, gore, self-harm, hate symbols or anything illegal.
  • No personal details in photos: phone numbers, names, addresses, social media handles or links.
  • Be kind. Report anything that feels wrong.

Moderation

Before a photo is sent, the app checks it on your phone for faces up close and for contact details; such photos cannot be sent. A person checks the first photos of every new account before anyone receives them; after that, photos from accounts in good standing go out without waiting, until one of them is reported. We may remove any photo and restrict or ban any account that breaks these rules, without notice. Reported photos are reviewed within 24 hours.

Your photos

You keep the rights to the photos you take. By sending a photo you allow us to store it and show it to another user, and you allow the person who receives it to keep it and use it for their own personal, non-commercial purposes (for example saving it or keeping a screenshot).

Age

You must be 18 or older to use Two Skies.
